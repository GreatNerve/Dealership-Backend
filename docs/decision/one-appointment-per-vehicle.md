# One Confirmed Appointment per Vehicle

Not “10 per person.” Not “100 per shop” (that fights ~100 bookings per dealer per day in the brief).

A Vehicle may have **one Confirmed Appointment**. Three cars, three bookings. Same car, 409, enforced by a partial unique index — same class of proof as duplicate Reminders. Cancelled and No-Show Expired do not block a rebook.
